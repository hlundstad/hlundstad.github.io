Frequently asked questions

The clock does not start when I arrive.
Automatic in and out needs “Always” location access: iPhone Settings → Zonio → Location → Always, with Precise Location on. Use a radius of at least 100 metres.
How do I track time without a fixed place?
Use the stopwatch in the Timeline: tap “Start new session”, type a tag and tap Start.
Where is my data?
Only on your phone. Turn on backup under the gear icon → Backup and choose a folder, for example in iCloud Drive, and you can restore everything on a new phone.
Can I get my hours into a spreadsheet?
Yes. Tap the share icon at the top of the Timeline (Export CSV). The file opens in Numbers, Excel or Google Sheets.
How do I add vacation or sick days?
Tap “+” in the Timeline and choose “Absence days …”.
