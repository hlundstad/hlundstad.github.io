Privacy

Zonio does not collect any information about you.

SupportPrivacy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Zonio is made by Hege Lundstad. This policy explains what information the app uses and where it is stored.

In short

Zonio does not collect any information about you. The app has no account, no server, no ads and no analytics or tracking tools. Everything you record stays on your phone, and in a backup only if you choose one yourself.

What the app stores, and where

  • Places: name, location (coordinates), radius and address, if any.
  • Sessions: when you arrived and left, duration and an optional note.
  • Settings: targets, working week, theme, language and similar.

This is stored only in the app's own storage on your phone. The developer has no access to it.

Location

The app uses your location for two things:

  • Automatic in and out (geofence). With “Always” access, your phone tells the app when you arrive at or leave a place you have added. The app stores only the time, not where else you have been. Location data is never sent from your phone.
  • Placing a new place. “Use my current location” gets your location once, so the place can be placed on the map.

You can change or turn off location access at any time in your phone's settings. The stopwatch and reports work without it.

Maps and addresses

The map and the address lookup for a place use the operating system's own services. On iPhone that is Apple (Apple Maps). On Android it is OpenStreetMap for the map and Google for address lookup. These services receive the map area or the coordinate that is shown or looked up, under their own privacy policies. Zonio sends nothing else.

Notifications

Notifications about arriving, leaving and reaching your daily target are created locally on your phone. They are not sent through any server.

Backup and export

If you turn on backup, your sessions and settings are written to a folder you choose, for example in your own iCloud Drive. If you export CSV, you decide where the file goes. The developer has no access to either.

Sharing

No information is shared with or sold to anyone.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not collect information about anyone.

Deletion

Delete a place or a session in the app, or delete the app. The data is then removed from your phone. A backup in a folder you chose is deleted by you, there.
